It refuses both the dark-terminal-plus-neon developer portfolio and the cream editorial portfolio: this page is a working inspection board, not a poster.

OWN-WORLD: Hi-vis orange fields, tar black, galvanized steel, chalk white, and webbing yellow. Load charts, job tags, inspection stamps, exposed rules, tabular numbers, 2px borders, no shadows, no gradients.
