## The Problem

The client's e-commerce checkout had a 68% drop-off rate. Users were adding items to cart but abandoning before completing payment. The existing checkout was a single page with 12 form fields — shipping, billing, payment, account creation — all visible at once.

User research showed that the wall of fields was overwhelming. Many users didn't want to create an account just to buy something. And the manual credit card entry was a friction point for mobile users.

## The Approach

I broke the checkout into 3 steps with a progress indicator: (1) shipping info, (2) payment, (3) review. Each step showed only the relevant fields, reducing cognitive load.

I added a guest checkout option — previously, account creation was required. This alone removed a major friction point. For users who did want accounts, I added an optional "Save this info for next time" checkbox after payment.

For payment, I replaced the custom credit card form with Stripe Checkout. This handled PCI compliance automatically and added Apple Pay / Google Pay support for mobile users. The integration took 2 days instead of the 2 weeks a custom solution would have required.

## The Result

Checkout drop-off fell from 68% to 45% — a 23% improvement. Mobile conversions increased by 31% (Apple Pay / Google Pay effect). The client reported that the new checkout "paid for itself in the first week."

React, Node.js, Stripe, PostgreSQL
